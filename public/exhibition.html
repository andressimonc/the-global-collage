The Local Collage

The Local Collage is where I hand the scissors to everyone else. At community events and activations, I invite the audience to build one collective collage around a theme we are living through together. It is the same belief as my own work, that we are made of fragments, turned into a shared experience. If you host events or run a space and want to bring this to your community, let’s talk.
