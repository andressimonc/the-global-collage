The Artist

Camila Castillo

Before words, there is feeling. That is where my art begins.

I’m Camila Castillo, a Venezuelan artist based in Miami. I came to visual art through a background in International Relations and intercultural communication, and that lens never left me. I think about how an image speaks across borders, not only how it looks. My influences are surrealism, a belief in everyday magic, and the artistic legacy of my grandfather, who taught me that making things is a way of thinking. Underneath all of it runs the value I care about most: global citizenship, the idea that empathy can cross any border, and that beauty built from many worlds can help us belong to each other.
