Commissions

Interested in a custom piece? Commissions are open on a limited basis. Each commission is a collaborative process: you bring the feeling, I find the image for it. All commissions are one-of-a-kind original collage works on paper, built from recycled magazine materials.

How it works

  1. 1

    You share the feeling and the details below.

  2. 2

    I send a direction and a quote.

  3. 3

    A deposit of half the agreed price secures your spot and begins the work. I make it by hand and ship it to you, insured, from Miami.
