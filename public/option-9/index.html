About

I’m Camila Castillo, a Venezuelan collage artist based in Miami. I was raised between languages and places, and I find collage serves perfectly to express the human experience because we are all assembled from fragments. My work lives where pop surrealism meets handmade, tactile collage, built to help us feel more and recognize each other across any border.
