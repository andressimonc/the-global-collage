# Option 3 — "Sticker Pop": the system

**The idea.** Her logo is a chunky die-cut sticker, so the whole site is a sticker sheet: full-width bands of her colours, and on them her art and the figures cut out of it, each with a thick white edge and a hard shadow, put down a little crooked. The visitor can pick the pieces up (the hero is a play table), because The Local Collage is where she "hands the scissors to everyone else".

Tokens, type, bands and components 1 to 8 are already in `public/option-3/css/site.css` and `js/site.js`: reuse the classes, do not restyle per page. The cart drawer and the page layouts below are still to build; their page-only rules go at the bottom of site.css under a `/* PAGE ONLY (file.html) */` comment, as Home's do. Header and footer markup: copy from `index.html` and move `aria-current="page"`.

## Colour
Tokens: `--cobalt #122FAB` · `--orange #FF5630` · `--green #496843` · `--paper #EFEDEA` · `--ink #231F20` · `--white #FFF`.
Every section is one `.band` plus one colour class. The class sets text, button and ring colour; never override them.

| Band | Text | `.btn` | Ring | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `.band--cobalt` | white | orange, ink text | orange | page openers, services |
| `.band--orange` | ink (never white) | cobalt, white text | white | people, calls to action |
| `.band--green` | white | paper, ink text | orange | walls of art and photos |
| `.band--paper` | cobalt | cobalt, white text | orange | type-led sections, lists, legal |
| `.band--world` | none (see below) | cobalt | white | product page, shop tiles |

Rules: stickers live on colour, type-led sections and long text live on paper. Two colours plus paper on any one screen. Neighbouring bands never share a colour. The footer is always cobalt, so the band above it is never cobalt. Hard shadows are always ink; sticker edges are always pure white. No gradients, no blur, no opacity tricks. Orange is never text on paper (lines and shapes only).
`.band--world`: put `style="--avg:#RRGGBB"` (the shop layer's `image.avg`) on the band; CSS lifts it (`oklch(from var(--avg) clamp(.45,l,.68) min(.17, c*2+.04) h)`). No text sits on that colour: words go in a `.panel` (paper, radius 22, shadow 6/7 ink) or under a sticker in a `.panel`.

## Type
Families from `../shared/fonts/fonts.css`. Headlines `"Neco"` 900 (never italic, never mixed into a sans line). Everything else `"TT Interphases"`. `"TT Interphases Mono"` only as `.label` (the drag hint, placeholders): at most one per screen.
- `.display.d1` clamp(3.1rem, 7.7vw, 7.75rem) / .86, the page h1 (and "The Work"). Phone hero: min(12.2vw, 5.75rem).
- `.display.d2` clamp(2.375rem, 4.6vw, 4.5rem) / .96, section headings. `.display.d3` clamp(1.625rem, 2.7vw, 2.5rem) / 1.
- `.lead` clamp(1.125rem, 1.45vw, 1.3125rem) / 1.45, weight 500. Body 1.0625rem / 1.6, 400. Buttons, nav, titles: 800.
- Letter-spacing −.012em on display, .06em on badges, .09em on labels. `text-wrap: balance` on display.
Pages whose copy gives no H1 use their menu word as the h1 (Shop, Services, Exhibition, Commissions, Contact) with the intro line as `.lead` beside it.

## Space and grid
`.wrap` = min(100% − 2 × gutter, 1320px). `--gutter` clamp(20px, 4.5vw, 72px). `.grid` = 12 equal columns, `--gap` clamp(16px, 2vw, 28px). Band padding `--band-pad` clamp(56px, 7vw, 104px) top and bottom. Headline to the first thing under it: clamp(24px, 3vw, 44px), never more. Full-bleed rows use `padding-inline: var(--edge)`. Text columns stop at 34em. Type and captions stay on the grid, straight; only stickers tilt.

## Components
1. **Band + orbit.** `<section class="band band--green"><span class="orbit" aria-hidden="true"></span>…`. The orbit is a 2px ellipse (3.6:1), clipped to its band, placed with `--orbit-x --orbit-y --orbit-w --tilt` (±7 to 12deg). One per band at most; route it behind pictures, not through body text. The planet is `<img class="planet" src="../shared/brand/icon-white-400.webp">` (black on paper/orange); it turns 16deg on hover. Never recolour it.
2. **Sticker.** `.sticker.sticker--cut` wraps a die-cut WebP from `img/stickers/` (white edge baked in by `docs/option-3/make-stickers.py`, shadow = one `drop-shadow(5px 6px 0 ink)`). `.sticker.sticker--rect` wraps a whole artwork or photo: `border: var(--border) solid #fff` (6 to 11px), radius 1.3 × border, `box-shadow: 5px 6px 0 ink`. Tilt with `style="--r:-2deg"`: artworks ±1 to 2deg, photos ±2 to 3deg, cut-outs ±3 to 9deg, alternating sign down a list. Inside a link it lifts on hover (−3px, −5px, tilt eases back, shadow grows to 9/11); outside a link add `.is-live` for the same lift. Stickers may overlap each other and a band's edge by up to a fifth; they never cover words or buttons.
3. **Button and link.** `.btn`: 58px tall, radius 16, weight 800, an arrow after the words, `box-shadow: 5px 5px 0 ink`; hover rises 2px, `:active` drops onto its shadow (translate 5px 5px, shadow 0) in .06s. `.btn--small` 46px (header). `.btn--plain` drops the arrow (Join, Cart, Menu, filters). One `.btn` per group; a second action is a `.link` (800, 3px underline in the band's button colour, arrow nudges 5px).
4. **Badge.** `.badge`: orange pill, ink caps .75rem 800, 3px white edge, 3px shadow, tilted −4deg; sits on a sticker's top corner, on the white edge. "Original, 1 of 1" everywhere a piece is shown for sale. `.badge--sold` (ink, white text) says "Sold" and replaces the buy button.
5. **Marquee.** `.proof` > `.proof__label` (a cobalt tab) + `.marquee` > two identical `ul.marquee__row` (second `aria-hidden`). Neco 900 names separated by the planet. 56s per loop, linear, pauses under the pointer; reduced motion shows one wrapped static row. The only looping motion on the site: one per page at most.
6. **Rows (`.doors`).** Full-bleed list on paper: 3px cobalt rules, each row `.door` = name in Neco (clamp 2.25rem, 5.6vw, 5.25rem), a `.door__note` lead, an arrow, and one cut-out sticker standing in the row and crossing its top rule. Hover fills the row cobalt. Use for doorways, the three services, the exhibition list, "how it works" (as `div.door` when the row is not a link; add a `.door--list` modifier for long lists: name at `.d3` size, note at body size, columns 5fr 6fr 1fr).
7. **Play table.** `[data-table]` on a band, `.table` layer inside it, each cut-out a `.piece[data-piece]` placed with `--x --y --w --r` (percent of the band). site.js makes them draggable (pointer events; centre stays in the band; picked piece comes to the front; on touch a vertical swipe still scrolls). `.table__hint.label` says "drag the pieces" and fades on first use. Home only.
8. **Fields and placeholders.** `.field` > `.field__label` (800) + `.input` (white, 58px, radius 16, inset 3px ink line; textarea min-height 160px). Forms always sit in a `.panel`. Anything Camila has not sent: `.needs-content` (3px dashed, mono caps) with plain words saying what is missing.

## Motion
One curve, `--ease: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`. Text: `data-reveal` fades in .6s and glides up 36px in 1.25s. Stickers: `data-reveal="stick"` on the sticker's parent, fades .45s while settling from scale 1.12 in 1.2s (a sticker being put down). Stagger with `style="--i:n"`, .07s each. Hovers .5s. Buttons press in .06s. Never put `data-reveal` on a `.btn` or `.sticker` itself (wrap it). Nothing bounces; only the marquee loops. Reduced motion: everything visible, nothing moves. Reveals arm only when JS runs (`html.js`).

## Phone (and the 700 to 960 pane)
Under 960px the nav becomes a Menu button (orange) beside Cart (cobalt); the menu is a full cobalt panel of Neco links. Under 760px bands stack to one column, the picture first when it is a person, text first otherwise; the hero's pieces move into their own zone under the buttons (82vw tall) so the whole hero fits the first screen; card grids go to two staggered columns; rows put the note under the name. Buttons are at least 46px tall; a lone `.btn` may run full width. No sideways scroll from 360px: every band clips x. Check 390, 760, 1000 and 1440.

## Cart (every page)
A drawer from the right, `aside.cart` (paper, 3px ink left edge, width min(440px, 100vw), slides in .6s on `--ease`; page behind covered by flat ink at 45%). Opens from `[data-cart-open]`, and after add-to-cart. Head: "Cart" in `.d3`, a Close `.btn--small.btn--plain`. Each line: the piece as a 76px `.sticker--rect` (tilt −2deg), title 800, "Original, 1 of 1", price, a Remove `.link` (no quantity control for originals). Foot: Subtotal row (800), "Ships from Miami, insured, US only.", a full-width `.btn` "Checkout" calling `cart.checkout()`. Empty: "Your cart is empty." and a `.btn` to shop.html. `[data-cart-count]` in the header shows `cart.count()`. Call `mountSampleNote()` wherever a price shows.
