# Option 7 "The Cover" — decisions (one line each; all of these are yours to change)

## Look
- Every section is a magazine cover: one flat colour, one giant word in Neco Black capitals across the whole width, a full-colour cut-out standing in front of it.
- Cover colours in order: cobalt (Home), orange (The Artist), off-white (The Work), green (Services), orange (Stay in touch), cobalt (The Deeper Why), near-black (footer).
- Giant words: orange on cobalt, cobalt on orange and off-white, off-white on green, near-black on the second orange, off-white on the second cobalt.
- The figure only covers the bottom fifth of the giant word, so the word always reads.
- No top bar. A slim off-white spine on the left edge holds the planet (home), one tick per cover, the cover's name, Cart and Menu; on a phone it lies along the bottom.
- The logo on the spine is the black planet icon; the full white logo is on the footer.
- Scrolling slides each cover up over the last; the giant word comes in from the side and the figure lands a moment later.

## Home
- Hero figure: the crowned woman cut out of "Tears Water The Soul - Silver". It is decoration; the whole piece is the first card in The Work.
- The headline is set as a small line ("I make worlds out of") above the giant word ("fragments.").
- Small line on the hero: "Handmade in Miami" (from the page description) and "Originals from $111" (the shop's price line), linking to the shop's originals.
- The exhibition names run along the foot of the first cover as a strip.
- About teaser: giant word "The Artist" (the About page's own heading); Camila's portrait cut out, in colour.
- The Work: giant words "The Work" (the copy's section label). Six originals, in this order: Tears Water The Soul - Silver, Senor Tigre, Zenda Beach, Gaga's Offering, The Art of Art, Let Out. All high resolution.
- Each of the six sits on a colour taken from its own picture: peach, dark olive, sky blue, plum, blue, red. Let Out's red is deepened a little so the white words on it read.
- Each card shows the sample price and an "Add to cart" button; adding opens the cart.
- Doorway links: Originals to the shop's originals, Prints & goods to the shop's prints, Commissions to the Commissions page.
- Services: giant word "Services"; the three services are the large lines. Figure: the three onlookers cut out of "Curiosity", pointing up at the word.
- Stay in touch: giant words from the copy's own label. Figure: the ink hand cut out of "Modern Love", reaching for the word.
- The Deeper Why: the copy gives no sentence for Home here, only the instruction and the button, so the cover is the heading, the framed Local Collage piece and the link "The Local Collage".
