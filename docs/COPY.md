### ARTIST STATEMENT (fixed block, word for word, same across all platforms)

I make collage and mixed media art that expresses abstract emotions through a pop surrealist lens. I want to translate the emotions that resist language into images anyone can recognize — work that connects people across cultures through our main commonality: feeling. Each piece is built from recycled magazines and found materials, cut, layered and reassembled, because that is the human experience: fragments that only make sense once they are aligned. My process is intuitive and admittedly a little messy. I let the imperfect show, because sincerity is the point.

— Camila Castillo, The Global Collage

### EXPANDED STORY

I'm Camila Castillo, a Venezuelan artist based in Miami. I came to visual art through a
background in International Relations and intercultural communication, and that lens never left
me. I think about how an image speaks across borders, not only how it looks. My influences are
surrealism, a belief in everyday magic, and the artistic legacy of my grandfather, who taught me
that making things is a way of thinking. Underneath all of it runs the value I care about most:
global citizenship, the idea that empathy can cross any border, and that beauty built from many
worlds can help us belong to each other.
