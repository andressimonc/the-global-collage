# Option 4 "The Poster" — decisions (one line each; all of these are yours to change)

## Look
- Ground is off-white; the giant words are orange or cobalt; whole sections also turn orange or cobalt. Order on Home: off-white hero, cobalt names strip, orange about, off-white work, cobalt services, off-white stay in touch, orange Local Collage, near-black footer.
- Giant words are the brand sans at its heaviest and narrowest; sentences are in Neco. The mono typeface is not used.
- Photos of Camila, her hands and her table are shown in black and white; her artworks are always in full colour.
- There is no top bar. A dock at the bottom centre holds Menu, Shop and Cart; the logo sits alone top-left, shrinks when you scroll and hides over the footer.
- Logo: the black V1 sticker on light sections, the white V1 on cobalt and in the footer.
- Each section holds on screen while the next one slides up over it; the one underneath shrinks a little and darkens.
- The exhibition names run across slowly by themselves (the one thing that moves without you); they stop under the pointer.
