# Site A "Saturn on the Mat" — decisions (one line each; all of these are yours to change)

## Where her notes were open
- Words on the green: headlines are big white type straight on the mat; every smaller word sits on a piece of paper (a slip or a card).
- The paper sections are sheets of paper lying on the mat with a strip of green showing each side (option 1), not full-width bands, so the mat is the ground everywhere.
- Section order: hero on the mat, names on paper, About on orange, the work on the mat, services on paper, stay in touch on the mat, the deeper why on paper, dark footer.
- The orange About is a sheet of orange paper that overlaps the bottom of the names sheet, like two papers on the table.
- About size: her name is about two thirds of option 4's (14.6% of the screen width instead of 22%); the portrait too (26% instead of 38%); it still stands in front of the end of "CASTILLO".
- About copy and button sit under her name on the left, as in option 4; on a phone the portrait comes under the name and the copy under a thin line.
- Logo: the V1 sticker; black over orange (as option 4 had it), hidden over the footer (the footer has its own), shrinks once you scroll (option 4).
- Option 9's portrait-in-a-circle with scraps riding round it is gone (replaced by option 4's About as asked); those scraps now lie on the mat.
- The cut-outs do not ride a ring: they slide in, drift with the scroll and move away from the pointer; the ring stays for her art.
- No ruler and no diagonal marks on the mat (round 2 ruled out rulers); just the grid and a soft light.
- Buttons: option 9's round-ended button; on the mat it is paper with a round hole showing the mat, on orange it is black (option 4's).
- The planet's words "Menu" and "Cart" sit on small paper pills so they read over the mat.
- The ring's arrows are paper discs with a soft shadow instead of outlined circles.
- Far pieces on the ring are tinted with the mat's green instead of option 9's blue.
- The names ring is a little narrower than in option 9 so a name at its side never leaves the paper.
- Stay in touch: her hands reach in from under the services sheet, cutting, with an eye and scissors beside them; the words and field are on a card.
- The cart opens over the table dimmed dark green (option 1), not blue.
- With JavaScript off the rings become plain rows and the logo stays white (it cannot change colour).
