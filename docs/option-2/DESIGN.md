# Option 2 — The Gallery Wall: the system

**The idea.** A calm gallery: paper walls, cobalt ink, her pieces framed, matted and shown whole on the black ledges they really hang on. One move does the collage work, taken from her statement: a picture arrives as cut fragments that glide into alignment.

Built reference: `public/option-2/index.html` + `css/site.css` + `js/site.js`. Copy a component's markup from Home; do not restyle it per page. Page-only rules go at the end of `site.css` under a `/* PAGE ONLY (file) */` comment.

## Colour (tokens in `:root`)
| Token | Value | Allowed for |
|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | `#EFEDEA` + `--grain` (inline SVG noise, alpha .18) | every wall; never flat white |
| `--mat` | `#FCFBF8` | mat board, wall labels, prints' borders, form fields |
| `--cobalt` | `#122FAB` (`--cobalt-deep #0B2082` on hover) | ALL type, buttons, rules, the painted wall (`.wall-cobalt`: proof strip, services, artist statement, footer). On cobalt, type is `--paper` |
| `--ink` | `#231F20` | black mouldings and the ledge only; shadows use `rgb(var(--sh) / a)`. Never for type |
| `--orange` | `#FF5630` | focus ring, sold dot, error dot, `.needs-content` outline. Never text, never a fill |
Green, mat green and marble blue are not used in this option.

## Spacing and grid
`.wrap`: max 1320px + gutters; `--gutter: clamp(20px, 4.5vw, 64px)`. `.section`: `padding-block: clamp(56px, 7.5vw, 112px)`; two paper sections in a row drop the second's top padding. `.split`: 12 equal columns, `--gap: clamp(16px, 2.4vw, 36px)`; picture spans 5, text spans 6 with one empty column between; picture side alternates down a page. Headline to first thing under it: 18–28px (`.stack`). `.section-head`: h2 left, its button right, on one baseline. Everything is square-cornered and unrotated at rest.

## Signature components
1. **Frame** `.framed` (+ `--white`, `--photo`): `padding: --mat-w clamp(14px, 2.3vw, 36px)` in `--mat`; `border: clamp(7px, .75vw, 11px) solid --ink` (white: `#F9F8F5`); four-layer drop shadow falling down-right; `::before` inset shadow = the moulding's lip on the mat; the picture gets `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #F1EEE8, 0 0 0 3px rgb(ink/.13)` (mat bevel). `--photo` = no mat, for photographs of people. Artworks are always matted, whole, true ratio.
2. **Fragments** `<span class="frag" data-frag="7|3" data-frag-on="load|view|hover"><img class="frag__base"></span>`: JS clones the image into N `clip-path: polygon()` pieces that tile the rectangle (cut tables `CUTS` in `site.js`). Start: each piece translated by its (dx, dy)% and rotated ±2.5–11°, opacity 0, with a drop shadow; then glide home. At rest the base image shows under the pieces (no seams). Under the pointer (`[data-frag-host]:hover`) pieces part by 11% of their scatter (3–8px) and close. **Budget per page: one `7` on load, one `3` on view, and `3` on hover for ledge cards. Nowhere else.**
3. **Wall label** `.label`: `--mat` card, 13/16px padding, small two-layer shadow, `width: fit-content`. Inside: `.label__title` (Neco 600 1.25rem, holds the link whose `::after` covers the whole hang), `.label__meta` (.875rem), `.label__mark` (mono caps "Original, 1 of 1"), `.label__price`, or `.label__sold` (orange dot + "Sold"). Never put a positioned box between `.label__title a` and its host.
4. **Picture ledge** `ul.ledge > li.piece > .piece__hang(.framed) + .piece__label(.label)`: 3 columns (2 under 720px), `.piece` is a 2-row subgrid so frames stand bottom-aligned on the shelf and labels hang under it; frames alternate black/white. The shelf is `.piece::before` on the first piece of each row: `100cqw + --bay` wide, 2px lit edge `#4A4546`, 12px `--ink` face, 32px shadow fading down. `.ledge--walk` (Home only): under 720px one sideways scroll-snap shelf, bays `min(68vw, 300px)` rounded to whole pixels, each piece drawing its own length of shelf.
5. **Niche** `.door__open` (the doorways): a picture detail (`object-fit: cover`, 5:4) recessed in the wall: `border: clamp(9px, 1.1vw, 16px) solid; border-color: #B9B5AF #F6F4F1 #FBFAF8 #CBC7C1` (four reveals lit from top-left) + inset shadow on the picture. Name in `.h3` size, one line under, arrow right. Details only; never a whole piece for sale.
6. **Painted wall + print** `.wall-cobalt` (cobalt + grain, paper type, `.btn--paper`) and `.print` (photo with a 6–10px `--mat` border and a hard shadow, unframed). Photographs of her studio and shows are prints; her artworks are framed.
7. **Ruled list** `.ruled`: Neco 600 `clamp(1.625rem, 1rem + 2.3vw, 3rem)` rows between 2px rules. Exhibitions use it with a large year (`.display` size) in the first 3 columns.
Also: `.btn` (54px, solid cobalt, square, arrow slides 6px), `.link-arrow` (2px underline), `.join` (input + button, one row), `.needs-content` (dashed orange outline, mono, says what is missing).

## Motion (one curve: `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`)
- Arrival `[data-reveal]`: 36px rise over 1.3s, opacity over .6s linear; `[data-stagger]` spaces children .07s. Armed only under `html.js`; a 3s guard in `<head>` disarms it if `site.js` never loads.
- Fragments: 1.3s per piece, .07s stagger, fade .45s; shadows drop at landing; cut lines heal .4s later. `index.html?frag=apart` holds the start position for review.
- Hover (.5s): nav underline grows, button darkens and its arrow slides, label title underlines, fragments part, niche picture eases from `scale(1.07)` to 1 (1.4s), header gains a shadow once scrolled. Nothing loops.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no pieces are built, everything is visible and still.
