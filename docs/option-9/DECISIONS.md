# Option 9 "The Orbit" — decisions (one line each; all of these are yours to change)

## Look
- Two grounds only: cobalt and off-white, alternating (cobalt hero, off-white names, cobalt about, off-white work, cobalt services, off-white stay in touch, cobalt Local Collage), then a near-black footer.
- All type is Neco in off-white or cobalt; the only other colour on the page is her art. No orange anywhere.
- There is no top bar. Her planet icon floats at the bottom centre: press it for the menu; the small moon on it is the cart and shows the count.
- The words "Menu" and "Cart" beside the planet show at the top of the page and tuck away once you scroll (they come back on hover).
- Logo: the white V1 sticker, alone top-left of the hero and in the footer; the black planet icon sits in the middle of the menu, the white one on the control.
- Pieces further round the ring are tinted with the background colour instead of being made see-through.

## Home
- Hero ring, twelve originals, in this order from the front going right: Be You, Artstract, The Art of Art, Let Out, Society, Gaga's Offering, Female Cactus, I Am the Bad Guy, Zenda Beach, Lucky Pleasures, Reflections of Me, Senor Tigre. All high resolution.
- Be You is at the front when the page opens, because pink reads strongest on the blue.
- The hero pieces are shown at one small file size (480 px wide) to keep the ring fast; the front one is a little soft on a very sharp screen.
- Under the front piece: its title and year, the word "Open", and "Drag to spin". Pressing any other piece brings it to the front; pressing the front one opens it.
- The headline breaks as "I make worlds / out of fragments." (three lines on a phone).
- Social proof: the four names ride a ring around the label and come round as you scroll; on a phone they are a plain list.
- About teaser: her portrait in a circle; four scraps from her table (two eyes, lips, scissors) go round it. "About" is a hidden heading for screen readers.
- Featured six, on a ring you can turn: Senor Tigre (front), Society, The Art of Art, Gaga's Offering, Female Cactus, Zenda Beach. Only the front one shows its price and its add button.
- Headings "The work" and "Ways to own it" come from the copy's own section label.
- Doorway discs: Originals shows a detail of Tears Water The Soul - Silver, Prints & goods a detail of Artstract, Commissions a detail of Feet on the Ground.
- Doorway links: Originals to the shop's originals, Prints & goods to the shop's prints, Commissions to the Commissions page.
- Services arm: each service is a line with a small moon going round it; "Services" is a hidden heading for screen readers.
- Stay in touch: heading from the copy's section label; the Join button is a round disc at the end of the field.
- The Deeper Why: the copy gives no sentence for Home here, only the instruction and the button, so the section is the heading "The deeper why", one Local Collage photo and the round button "The Local Collage".
- Local Collage picture: the wide framed collage on the block wall (the first photo in the folder), not the easel one.

## Cut-outs
- No figure was cut out of an artwork. The four scraps round her portrait (two eyes, lips, scissors) are from her table photos, from the shared cut-out library. None were made for this option.
- The three doorway discs crop an artwork to a circle as a detail; the whole piece is always shown on its card.

## Words added (the copy does not give them)
- Control: "Menu", "Cart", "Close". Marks on unbuilt pages and legal links: "soon".
- Hero ring: "Open", "Drag to spin"; the arrows read "Previous piece" and "Next piece" to screen readers. Featured ring: "Drag to turn".
- Cart: "Cart", "Your cart is empty.", "Subtotal", "Checkout", "Remove", "Close"; the empty cart links with the copy's "Explore the collection". The shipping line in the cart is the copy's.
- Cards: "Sold" on a sold piece; the add button reads "Add [title] to cart" to screen readers.
- Newsletter fields are labelled "Email"; the button is the copy's "Join". "Skip to content" for keyboards.
- Footer line is set without the trailing comma: "Based in Miami, available in the US".
- The apostrophe in "I'm" is typographic; no other character changed.

## Links and behaviour
- @theglobalcollage links to instagram.com/theglobalcollage (address assumed from the handle).
- In the menu and footer only Home is a link; About, Shop, Services, Exhibition, Commissions, Contact, Shipping & Returns and Privacy are marked "soon" and are not links.
- Only Home is built in this option, so every piece and every button to another page lead to the review site's "not built yet" page.
- On a short screen (a real phone in Safari, a laptop inside the review site) the hero is now a little taller than the screen: the ring keeps its size, the planet rides over the foot of the front piece, and the arrows sit just below the fold. Before, the front piece covered "Work with me" on a phone and the planet landed on the ring's caption on a laptop. On a tall screen nothing changed.
- The planet steps aside: when a headline, a button, a price or a field comes under it, it slides below the screen edge (as it already does for the cart) and returns once the spot is clear. It used to sit on card titles, prices and the email field.
- The sample-price note sits in the page beside "The work" (it used to float top-right over whatever scrolled by) and can be closed. The checkout note leaves when the cart closes.
- Sold pieces stay on show with a "Sold" mark, no price and no add button.
- With reduced motion switched on, every ring is laid out and still; the arrows and drag still turn it, without the glide.
- With JavaScript off the rings fall back to plain rows and grids, and the planet control is not shown (the footer has the full navigation).

## Placeholders
- Nothing on Home is a placeholder. Prices and sizes are the shop layer's samples and say so in the small note.
