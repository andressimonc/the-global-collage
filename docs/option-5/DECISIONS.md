# Option 5 "The Archive" — decisions (one line each; all of these are yours to change)

## Look
- The page is dark: a near-black table (#161314) with one soft light from the top left. Type is paper white, in Neco.
- Orange is used for one thing only, "act": the main buttons, the add-to-cart control, focus and form errors.
- No top bar. Four plain words in the corners: logo top left, Shop top right, Menu bottom left, Cart bottom right.
- On a phone the logo scrolls away with the page and Menu, Shop and Cart sit along the bottom edge.
- The logo is the white V1 sticker, with a soft shadow like every other object on the table.
- Her pieces are always shown as prints with a white border and a caption underneath, slightly tilted; they straighten when you point at them.
- The cart and the artist statement are sheets of paper (her off-white, dark type) laid on the table.

## Home
- Hero piece: none. The hero is nine of her cut-outs around the headline, and every one can be picked up and moved.
- The nine: her hands cutting on the mat, the framed "Candy", the walking tiger, the lips, the crowned head, the cactus, the butterfly on the flower, a loose magazine eye, loose magazine lips. On a phone seven of them show.
- The headline breaks as "I make worlds / out of fragments." (three lines on a phone).
- The exhibition names are one large line that travels sideways as you scroll (a plain stacked list on a phone).
- About teaser: the About portrait, as a print. No caption on it.
- Featured six, in this order: Senor Tigre, Be You, The Art of Art, Artstract, Gaga's Offering, Zenda Beach (all high resolution, chosen for colour against the dark table).
- Featured cards show the price and an "Add to cart" control, not just a link.
- Featured prints are three sizes on desktop, not three equal columns; on a phone they are one row to flick through.
- Doorway links: Originals to the shop's originals, Prints & goods to the shop's prints, Commissions to the Commissions page.
- Doorway pictures: "Reflections of Me" in its frame, a fan of finished collages on the floor, and "Feet on the Ground".
- "See how we can work together" is an outlined button, so "See everything in the shop" stays the one orange button near it.
- The Local Collage: the framed piece from the exhibition (cut out of its wall photo) with the easel photo of #3 The Butterfly Effect on its corner. No body text, because the copy gives none for Home.
- "Stay in touch" picture: the sketchbook collage in progress (it became The Art of Art).

## Links and behaviour
- @theglobalcollage links to instagram.com/theglobalcollage (address assumed from the handle).
- Buttons to pages not built yet (Services, Commissions, Exhibition) already point at those pages, so they lead nowhere until the pages exist.
- On a phone a swipe up or down on an object scrolls the page; a sideways drag moves the object.
- Adding a piece opens the cart. In this preview the cart shows the sample-price line inside it.
- On a phone the preview note about sample prices sits at the top, beside the logo. It used to sit above the three bottom words, where it covered "Work with me" on shorter phones (2026-10-13).
- With reduced motion switched on, or JavaScript off, everything is simply in place and nothing moves.
